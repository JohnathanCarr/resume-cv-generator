Settings

Create a key at platform.openai.com/api-keys. It is stored only in this browser and sent directly to OpenAI when you verify or generate. Once saved it can be replaced but not viewed.

Before you generate

  • Generation is billed to your OpenAI account at OpenAI's API prices. Resume Studio itself is free and charges nothing.
  • Your use of the API is subject to OpenAI's usage policies.
  • AI can get things wrong. Read every resume and cover letter, and check names, dates and numbers, before you send it.

Keywords not in your profile

The posting's key requirements include these, and your profile does not mention them. Check the ones you actually have; they are added to your skills so the resume can use the posting's exact wording.
