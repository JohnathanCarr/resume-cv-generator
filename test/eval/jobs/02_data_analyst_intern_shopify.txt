Data Analyst Intern (Summer 2027)
Shopify · Remote (North America)

Shopify's Merchant Analytics team turns billions of commerce events into decisions for millions of merchants. As a Data Analyst Intern you'll work with analysts and data scientists to answer real business questions, build dashboards that teams actually use, and learn how experimentation works at scale.

Responsibilities
- Write SQL against large event datasets to answer product and business questions
- Build and maintain dashboards (we use Looker and Tableau) for merchant-facing teams
- Support A/B test analysis and communicate results to non-technical stakeholders
- Clean and validate data pipelines; flag data quality issues early

Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Informatics, Computer Science, Economics or a related field, graduating 2027 or later
- Proficiency in SQL and Python (pandas)
- Experience with a data visualization tool (Tableau, Looker, Power BI)
- Understanding of basic statistics and A/B testing
- Clear written communication
- Bonus: experience with R, dbt, or a prior analytics internship
