Machine Learning Engineer
Lumen Health · Austin, TX (On-site)

Requirements
- 2+ years of applied ML in production, or an MS with a strong project record
- Strong Python; experience with scikit-learn and TensorFlow or PyTorch
- Experience building retrieval-augmented generation systems (embeddings, vector search, reranking)
- Familiarity with data engineering: pandas, SQL, batch pipelines
- Comfort working in a regulated environment (HIPAA); we'll teach the specifics

Nice to have
- Experience with LangChain, Pinecone, or MongoDB Atlas Vector Search
- Cloud deployment experience (Azure preferred)
- Experience with model monitoring and evaluation frameworks
