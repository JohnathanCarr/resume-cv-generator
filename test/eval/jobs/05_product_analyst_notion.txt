Product Analyst
Notion · New York, NY or Remote

Notion is looking for a Product Analyst to partner with our Growth team. You'll define metrics, run experiments, and turn messy product data into decisions about onboarding, activation, and retention.

Responsibilities
- Define and instrument product metrics with engineering and PMs
- Design and analyze experiments; write up results for leadership
- Build self-serve dashboards and maintain metric definitions in dbt
- Investigate anomalies in usage data and explain what happened

Requirements
- 2+ years in product analytics, data analysis, or a related role
- Expert SQL; comfortable with Python for analysis
- Experience with experimentation (A/B testing, causal inference basics)
- Experience with a BI tool (Mode, Looker, Tableau) and with dbt
- Strong written communication; you can explain a result to a VP in one page

Nice to have
- Experience at a product-led growth company
- Experience mentoring or onboarding analysts
